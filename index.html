🎉 AUCTION WON

A Historic Moment for Our Community — Time to Act

With divine blessings and the strength of our community, we are pleased to share that we have successfully won the auction for our future Hindu Community Centre & Mandir.

This is a proud milestone for all of us. What began as a shared vision has now become a real and tangible step forward.

Opportunities to contribute towards the development of a Mandir in London do not come often. This is a rare chance for our generation to help create something lasting — a sacred Hindu space.

As we now move into the completion and funding stage:

If you have not yet pledged, we invite you to come forward and donate.

If you have already pledged, please consider increasing your contribution if you are able.

Let us continue together and build something that will serve our families and future generations. 🙏

Welcome to SHCS

A local community group formed by and for the people of Hindu beliefs in Sutton and neighbouring areas

🕉️

Our Vision

To promote the principles and common understandings of the 'Sanatan Hindu Dharma' among the members and society.

🙏

Our Mission

Provide a common platform for worship, prayer, cultural functions, and support for the Hindu community in Sutton.

🤝

Community

Serving an estimated 8,000 Hindu residents in Sutton with cultural, religious, and social programs.
