Where it goes

How your donation is used

Donations are used for ongoing temple operations: festival celebrations, religious ceremonies and community activities. Any surplus is saved towards our ultimate goal — a permanent place of worship for the Hindu community in Sutton. Donations towards the Mandir Appeal fund the fit-out, establishment and development of the Temple and Community Centre.

Gift Aid

Gift Aid — add 25% at no cost to you

If you are a UK taxpayer, SHCS can reclaim 25p for every £1 you donate, at no extra cost to you. That adds £250 to every £1,000 given. Please complete a Gift Aid declaration so we can claim it.

Higher-rate taxpayers can claim back a further 20–25% through Self Assessment. Limited companies may instead deduct charitable donations from taxable profits for Corporation Tax purposes, subject to HMRC guidelines.

Larger gifts

Larger gifts and interest-free loans

Families donating £500 or more, and patrons providing interest-free loans of £5,000 or more, can have their family names engraved in the Mandir – Community Centre. Details of both routes, including the pledge form, are on the appeal page.
