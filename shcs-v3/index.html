A Mandir for Sutton

The Sanatan Hindu Cultural Society serves an estimated 8,000 Hindu residents of Sutton and its neighbouring areas. We have secured a property for our permanent Mandir and Community Centre. Now we are raising £200,000 to complete it.

The Society

Welcome to SHCS

Sanatan Hindu Cultural Society is a community group formed by and for people of Hindu beliefs in Sutton and the neighbouring areas. Our purpose is simple. We promote the principles and common understandings of Sanatan Hindu Dharma. We provide a common platform for worship, prayer, and cultural and religious functions. We are a UK Registered Charity, number 1197115.

The Mandir Appeal

The auction is won. The work begins.

With the blessings of the community, SHCS has successfully won the auction for our future Hindu Community Centre and Mandir, near Carshalton Beeches station. One hundred per cent of the bid amount has been secured, subject to legal completion, and the legal completion process is underway.

Our new fundraising target is £200,000. It will pay for the fit-out, establishment and development of the Temple and Community Centre once the acquisition is complete. Opportunities to help build a Mandir in London do not come often. This is our generation's chance to create a lasting sacred space.

Bid amount: 100% secured, subject to legal completion Fit-out target: £200,000
