Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Sutton Mandir

Our commitment to your privacy

The Sanatan Hindu Cultural Society (SHCS) is committed to protecting the privacy of our website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use our website.

Information collection

We may collect personal information from you when you fill out a form or make a donation on our website. This information may include your name, email address, mailing address and phone number. We may also collect information about your browsing behaviour on our website.

Use of information

We may use your personal information to contact you about events, updates and other information related to SHCS. We may also use your information for research and analysis to improve our website and services.

Sharing of information

We will not share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent.
  • As required by law.
  • To protect against fraud or other illegal activities.
  • With service providers who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or serving you.

Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and have implemented appropriate measures to protect it from unauthorised access, use or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies

We may use cookies to personalise your experience on our website and improve its performance. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may limit your use of certain features on our website.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to change this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on our website.

Contact us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@suttonmandir.org.uk.
