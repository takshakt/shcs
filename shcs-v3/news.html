News

Reports from our festivals and community work.

  • 24 March 2024

    Rang Barse: Holi fills Manor Park with colour

    On 24 March, SHCS held its annual Rang Barse Holi celebration at Manor Park. More than 1,000 people from Sutton and beyond attended. The day marked the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil, with DJ music, dhol drumming, and unlimited colours for everyone to play with.

    The Mayor of Sutton attended, along with several Sutton council representatives. Their presence reflected what the event has become: a fixture in the borough's calendar and a point of connection between communities. Rang Barse is more than a festival. It is a celebration of shared heritage, and of the bonds that hold a community together.

  • 24 October 2023

    Ravan Dahan at Rose Hill Park draws 5,000 people

    On the evening of 24 October, SHCS hosted its Ravan Dahan celebration at Rose Hill Park. Around 5,000 people attended. The centrepiece was the ceremonial burning of a 25-foot Ravan effigy, marking the victory of good over evil, followed by a professional fireworks display. Indian street food stalls served traditional dishes throughout the evening.

    The Mayor of Sutton, Councillor Colin Stears, and Council Leader Ruth Dombey were guests of honour. Both spoke of the event's inclusive spirit and its role in promoting cultural understanding within the borough. The evening was made possible by SHCS volunteers and sponsors, whose work covered everything from crafting the effigy to running the programme.

  • 23 February 2021

    COVID charity food donations

    On 23 February 2021, SHCS delivered 70 food boxes to three groups serving Sutton through the pandemic:

    • The COVID ward at St Helier Hospital, at the forefront of the battle against the virus.
    • Sutton Night Watch, "The Light In The Shadows", supporting homeless people in Sutton.
    • Sutton Police, working to keep the neighbourhood safe.

    The boxes contained nutritious meals prepared and delivered with gratitude. The initiative reflected the spirit of seva — selfless service — that guides the Society's work, in difficult times as in good ones.
