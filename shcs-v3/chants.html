Chants & Prayers

The aartis, stotrams, mantras and bhajans we sing together, gathered in one place for use at home and at our gatherings.

About this collection

Introduction

This collection holds the devotional texts used at SHCS events. Each chant can be read in full on this page, and the complete Aarti and Bhajans booklet can be downloaded as a PDF.
