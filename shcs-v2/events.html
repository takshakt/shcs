Recurring Event

Weekly Satsang

Join us every Sunday evening for prayers, bhajans, and community fellowship

When & Where

Every Sunday
7:00 PM - 9:00 PM
Rosehill Pavillion Hall
Rosehill Park
Sutton SM1 3HH

All are welcome to join us for our weekly gathering. The satsang includes prayers, devotional songs (bhajans), and community time. It's a wonderful opportunity to connect with fellow community members and strengthen your spiritual practice.

Festival Celebrations

We celebrate all major Hindu festivals throughout the year

🎨

Holi

Spring (March)

Festival of Colors celebrating the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil. Our annual Rang Barse event at Manor Park attracts over 1000 attendees.

🏹

Ram Navami

Spring (March/April)

Celebrating the birth of Lord Rama. Special prayers, kirtans, and prasad distribution.

🎂

Janmashtami

Summer (August)

Lord Krishna's birthday celebration with fasting, prayers, and joyous festivities including dahi handi.

💃

Navratri

Autumn (September/October)

Nine nights of worship to Goddess Durga with garba, dandiya, and devotional celebrations.

🔥

Dussehra

Autumn (October)

Victory of good over evil. Our Ravan Dahan event features a spectacular 25-foot effigy burning and fireworks display.

🪔

Diwali

Autumn (October/November)

Festival of Lights celebrating the return of Lord Rama. Prayers, diyas, fireworks, and community feast.

🔱

Maha Shivratri

Winter (February/March)

Great night of Lord Shiva with night-long prayers, fasting, and abhishekam ceremonies.

🐘

Ganesh Chaturthi

Summer/Autumn (August/September)

Birthday of Lord Ganesha with special pujas, modak offerings, and community celebrations.

🙏

Hanuman Jayanti

Spring (March/April)

Celebrating Lord Hanuman's birth with Hanuman Chalisa recitations and special prayers.
