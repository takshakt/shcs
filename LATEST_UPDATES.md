### Why This Approach?

Instead of creating 19 separate HTML pages (or 19 separate PDFs), we use one comprehensive PDF that contains all the chants. This:
- ✅ Is much easier to maintain
- ✅ Gives users all chants in one download
- ✅ Reduces page load and maintenance
- ✅ Preserves the original formatting from the old site
