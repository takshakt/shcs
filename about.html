About Sanatan Hindu Cultural Society, Sutton

Sanatan Hindu Cultural Society (SHCS) is a local community group formed by and for the people of Hindu beliefs in the Sutton and neighbouring areas.

There are estimated 8,000 Hindu people living in Sutton and neighbouring areas with no common platform/place for performing worship, prayer, cultural and religious functions. In order to fulfil this cultural & religious requirement, Sanatan Hindu Cultural Society (SHCS) was formed.

Our Vision

To promote the principles and common understandings of the 'Sanatan Hindu Dharma' among the members and society.

Our Mission

Four key priorities guiding our work in the community

🛕

Common Platform

To provide a common platform for the people of Hindu beliefs in the Sutton and neighbouring areas for performing prayer, worship and cultural & religious functions.

📿

Religious Guidance

To provide guidance and facilities for religious functions and performing of all religious rites connected with births, marriages and deaths.

🌍

Environmental Awareness

To create awareness about Global Warming and encouraging people to achieve goals set by UK government to achieve Zero net carbon emission.

🤝

Community Integration

Help newly arriving Hindus in the area to integrate with the wider British society.

Our Objectives

Specific goals we work towards every day

📖

Philosophical Discussions

To facilitate discussions on concepts of Hinduism as per various schools of Hindu philosophy and the prime values of the Hindu way of life.

🧘

Wellbeing Programs

To organise Yoga, Sports and Meditation workshops for physical and mental wellbeing.

💝

Charitable Events

To organise charitable events for fund raising towards natural calamities around the world and support local charities of similar cause.

🌱

Environmental Education

To create Environmental awareness and effects of the Global Warming by holding info workshops and seminars.
