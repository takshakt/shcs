Frequently Asked Questions

Is SHCS a registered charity?

Yes. SHCS is a UK Registered Charity (No. 1197115).

What tax benefit do individual donors get?

Donations may be eligible for Gift Aid, allowing the charity to reclaim tax on qualifying donations. Higher rate taxpayers can claim back 20-25% via Self Assessment.

What about business donations?

Limited companies may deduct charitable donations from taxable profits for Corporation Tax purposes, subject to HMRC guidelines.

What is an interest-free loan?

A refundable loan given to the charity to help purchase the property. No interest is paid. Governed by formal legal agreement.

When will loans be repaid?

Current target repayment period is 6-8 years, subject to charity's financial position and available funds from donations and Mandir income.

What if the purchase doesn't proceed?

Donations: Will remain part of SHCS's charitable funds for future Mandir opportunity. Pledges: Will not be requested and may be withdrawn.
